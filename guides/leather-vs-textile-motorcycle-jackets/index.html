Leather vs Textile Motorcycle Jackets

Leather motorcycle jackets give the best abrasion resistance and a close sport fit, so they suit track, sport and street riding. Textile jackets are lighter, more weatherproof and better ventilated, so they suit touring, adventure and commuting. Both can carry the same CE armour.

Which should a new brand launch with?

Launch where your riders are. Track and sport brands should lead with leather race suits and sport jackets. Adventure and touring brands should lead with a textile jacket and trouser set. Many brands sell both: a leather sport jacket for summer and a textile all-season jacket.

Specification checklist

  • Leather: cowhide thickness, finish, perforation zones, stretch panels.
  • Textile: shell fabric, waterproof membrane or liner, thermal liner, reflective panels.
  • Both: armour level per zone, back pocket, zip connection to trousers, branding.

FAQ

Is leather or textile better for track days?

Leather. Leather race suits and sport jackets are the standard choice for track riding because of their abrasion resistance and close fit.

Can textile jackets include CE armour?

Yes. Armour level does not depend on the shell material; textile jackets take the same EN 1621-1 inserts when the pockets are designed for them.

Are textile motorcycle jackets waterproof?

They can be, with a laminated waterproof membrane or a removable waterproof liner, chosen at the sample stage.
