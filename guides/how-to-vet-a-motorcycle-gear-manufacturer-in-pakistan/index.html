How to Vet a Motorcycle Gear Manufacturer in Pakistan

To vet a motorcycle gear manufacturer in Pakistan, confirm the company and factory exist, ask for a paid sample before bulk, get MOQ, price, lead time and terms in a written quote, request armour documentation, take a live video tour, start with a small run and use traceable payments.

Last updated 31 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

Why Sialkot

Sialkot in Punjab is one of the world's best-known centres for leather sportswear and protective gear. The city has a deep supply base of leather, trims and skilled stitchers, and many international motorcycle brands have manufactured there. That depth also means quality varies widely, so vetting matters.

The 10-point checklist

  1. Company identity. A registered business name, a physical factory address and a working company email domain.
  2. Live video tour. Ask to see cutting, stitching and finishing in real time.
  3. Paid sample first. A serious factory samples before bulk and lets you approve in writing.
  4. Written quote. MOQ per style, unit price, sample fee, lead times, payment terms and Incoterm.
  5. Armour documents. EN 1621 certificates for the inserts used.
  6. Honest certification claims. Beware "CE certified suit" claims without an EN 17092 certificate.
  7. Material specifics. Leather type and thickness, thread, zips and lining named on the spec.
  8. Inspection. Bulk checked against the approved sample before shipping; third-party inspection allowed.
  9. Traceable payments. Bank transfer to a company account or a secure checkout; avoid personal accounts.
  10. IP terms. You keep your logos and artwork; pattern ownership agreed in writing.

FAQ

Is Sialkot good for motorcycle gear manufacturing?

Yes. Sialkot is a major centre for leather sportswear and protective gear with a deep supply base, but quality varies between factories, so always sample first.

How do I avoid scams when sourcing from Pakistan?

Confirm the registered company and factory address, take a live video tour, order a paid sample, get a written quote and pay only to a company account or secure checkout.
