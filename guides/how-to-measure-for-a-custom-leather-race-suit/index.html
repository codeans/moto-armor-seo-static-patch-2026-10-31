How to Measure for a Custom Leather Race Suit

To measure for a custom leather race suit, take chest, waist, hip, thigh, knee, calf, inseam, outside leg, sleeve, shoulder width, back length and height, ideally with a helper and wearing the base layer you ride in. Measure the arms and legs bent as on the bike so the suit fits in a riding position.

Last updated 31 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

Before you start

  • Use a soft tape measure and a helper. Self-measuring the back and shoulders is unreliable.
  • Wear the base layer you will ride in. Note its thickness on the brief.
  • Keep the tape snug, not tight. Record in centimetres.
  • Measure twice; if the two readings differ, use the average.

The 12 measurements

Race suit measurement guide
#MeasurementHow to take it
1HeightBarefoot, against a wall.
2ChestAround the fullest part, under the arms, arms relaxed.
3WaistAround the natural waist, above the navel.
4HipAround the widest part of the seat.
5Shoulder widthAcross the back, shoulder bone to shoulder bone.
6Back lengthFrom the base of the neck to the waist.
7SleeveShoulder bone, over a slightly bent elbow, to the wrist bone.
8ThighAround the widest part of the upper thigh.
9KneeAround the knee with the leg slightly bent.
10CalfAround the widest part of the calf.
11InseamCrotch to the floor, standing straight.
12Outside legWaist to the floor along the side of the leg.

Fit tips for a riding position

  • Race suits are pre-curved: arms bent forward, knees bent. A suit that feels slightly short in the arms when standing is often right on the bike.
  • Leather stretches a little with use, so the first fitting should feel snug.
  • Armour must sit on the joint. Check knee and elbow position in a tuck.
  • If you wear a back protector or airbag vest, tell us so we size the back.

Measuring a team

For team and club orders, collect each rider's 12 measurements on one sheet with their name and number. We sample a mid size of the team design, then build each rider's suit to their measurements, or grade a standard size run if you prefer. See custom team race suits.

Standard sizes

Our standard race-suit sizes run EU 46–60 with US and UK equivalents. Use standard sizes for retail stock and made-to-measure for riders who race.

FAQ

Should I measure over base layers?

Yes. Measure as you will ride, and note the base layer thickness on the brief so the sample accounts for it.

What if my team has mixed sizes?

Send each rider's measurements. We sample one size of the team design, then build each suit to measure or grade a size run.

What sizes do your standard race suits come in?

EU 46–60 with US and UK equivalents, plus made-to-measure.
