What Does It Cost to Manufacture a Custom Leather Race Suit?

The unit cost of a custom leather race suit depends mainly on the leather (cowhide or kangaroo), armour level, construction details, branding, order quantity and freight. At Moto Armor a paid sample starts from USD 189 before freight, and the written quote gives the exact bulk unit price for your specification and quantity.

Last updated 31 Oct 2026 · 2 min read

The seven cost drivers

What moves the unit price of a leather race suit
DriverLower costHigher cost
Leather1.2–1.4 mm cowhideKangaroo panels or full kangaroo
ArmourCE Level 1 insertsCE Level 2 limbs and back
ConstructionTwo-piece, standard panelsOne-piece, aero hump, perforation zones, many stretch panels
Colour and graphics2–3 colours, few panelsMany colour blocks, printed graphics
BrandingOne printed chest logoMultiple embroidered logos and sponsor patches
SizingGraded standard sizesMade-to-measure per rider
QuantityLarger runs per styleMinimum runs (from 10 one-piece suits)

Costs outside the unit price

  • Sample fee: from USD 189 before freight, due before cutting. It is not a deposit on bulk unless your quote says so.
  • Freight: courier for samples; air or sea freight for bulk, quoted by destination.
  • Duties and import taxes: paid by the buyer unless the quote states a delivered-duty-paid term.
  • Certification: EN 17092 garment testing at a notified body is a separate cost for the brand. See EN 17092 classes.

How to get an accurate quote fast

  1. Pick a base design from the race suit catalogue or send your own reference.
  2. State leather, armour level per zone and suit type (one-piece or two-piece).
  3. Attach logo files and a colour reference.
  4. Give a quantity and size split and the destination country.

Send that brief through the quote form or to sales@getmotoarmor.com and we reply with a written quote covering the sample fee, unit price, MOQ, lead time and shipping terms.

FAQ

How much does a custom leather race suit sample cost?

Samples start from USD 189 before freight at Moto Armor. The exact fee depends on the design complexity and is stated on your quote before cutting.

Is kangaroo leather more expensive than cowhide?

Yes. Kangaroo is lighter and more supple for similar abrasion resistance, and it costs more per suit. Many brands use cowhide with kangaroo in selected panels.

Who pays import duties on gear from Pakistan?

The buyer, unless the quote states a delivered-duty-paid term.
