Race Suits FAQ

Can armour be specified as CE rated?

Yes. You can specify CE EN 1621-1 Level 1 or Level 2 armour inserts. That rating applies to the armour component, not automatically to the whole garment.

Do you provide size charts?

Yes. Each category can link a size chart. Custom programs can grade from your chart or a fitted sample.

How do I measure for a race suit?

Measure chest, waist, hip, inseam, sleeve and height in a riding posture. See our measuring guide for detail.

Can you grade a full size run from one sample?

Yes. Sample a mid size, approve fit, then we grade the bulk size breakdown you provide.

Which leathers do you offer?

Cowhide and kangaroo are common for race programs. Goatskin is used on gloves and flexible zones.

Do you work with Cordura and mesh?

Yes. Textile jackets and summer gloves use Cordura and mesh with armour pockets as specified.

What is the minimum order for custom leather race suits?

One-piece leather race suits start from 10 units per style and other suits from 50 units per style, after you approve a paid sample. Your written quote confirms the exact minimum for your design.

How long does it take to make a custom race suit sample?

About 12–18 days after the sample fee is paid, plus freight to your country. Bulk production takes about 30–45 days after written sample approval.

Cowhide or kangaroo leather for a race suit?

Cowhide in 1.2–1.4 mm is the durable, cost-effective standard for track and road suits. Kangaroo is lighter and more supple for the same abrasion resistance, and costs more. We can sample both so you can compare weight and feel.

Can you make race suits under my own brand?

Yes. Private-label suits carry your logos, labels and packaging and are not marked as Moto Armor products unless you ask. You keep the rights to your artwork.
